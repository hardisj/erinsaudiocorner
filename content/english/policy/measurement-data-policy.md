**Erin's Audio Corner | Erin's Audio Guide**

**Effective Date: October 29, 2026**

## 1. Introduction

The speaker measurements, reviews, graphics, analysis, and associated materials published by Erin's Audio Corner and Erin's Audio Guide represent original work produced by Erin Hardison.

Producing these measurements requires substantial investments in specialized equipment, time, research, and analysis. This information is made available to help consumers better understand audio equipment, evaluate product performance, and make informed purchasing decisions.

I encourage people to learn from, discuss, and reference my work. However, making this information publicly accessible does not mean that I am granting unrestricted permission to collect, reproduce, redistribute, or commercially exploit protected portions of that work.

This policy explains the intended uses of my measurement data and associated content, as well as the restrictions that apply to their use.

## 2. Permitted Uses

Visitors are welcome to:

- View and interact with published measurements for personal research, education, and product evaluation.
- Share links to original reviews, measurement pages, and other content published by Erin's Audio Corner.
- Reference measurement results in discussions, commentary, independent reviews, and educational materials.
- Discuss and compare audio products using information obtained from my reviews.
- Quote or reproduce limited portions of protected content where authorized or permitted under applicable law, including fair use.

When referencing my work, attribution to Erin Hardison / Erin's Audio Corner and a link to the original review are appreciated.

These permitted uses do not grant unrestricted access to or redistribution rights in underlying measurement datasets.

## 3. Restricted Uses

Except where expressly authorized in writing by Erin Hardison, or otherwise permitted by applicable law, the following activities are prohibited.

### 3.1 Automated Scraping and Data Harvesting

Using bots, crawlers, scripts, automated tools, or other methods to systematically collect, download, or extract measurement data, graphics, or other protected content from this website.

This includes automated collection intended to populate or maintain third-party measurement databases, product comparison services, ranking systems, or commercial websites.

### 3.2 Graph Digitization and Numerical Data Extraction

Using image-processing software, graph digitizers, optical character recognition (OCR), AI-based image analysis, curve-tracing algorithms, or similar techniques to extract, estimate, or reconstruct numerical measurement data from published graphs, images, screenshots, videos, or interactive visualizations for the purpose of building, distributing, or commercially exploiting third-party measurement datasets.

This restriction is intended to address both automated and manual methods of reconstructing measurement datasets from published graphics.

### 3.3 Measurement Database Replication

Systematically collecting, reconstructing, aggregating, or repackaging measurement data originating from Erin's Audio Corner to create, populate, maintain, or enhance third-party measurement databases, comparison tools, ranking systems, or commercial services without authorization.

This includes using extracted measurements to generate replacement graphics, calculated specifications, or derivative datasets for redistribution or commercial exploitation, to the extent such activities are subject to enforceable legal restrictions.

### 3.4 Commercial Redistribution

Reproducing, republishing, licensing, selling, or otherwise commercially exploiting protected measurement graphics, datasets, reviews, or substantial portions of the website's original protected content without authorization.

This includes use in commercial measurement aggregation services, affiliate-supported product comparison websites, and other revenue-generating platforms where the use requires permission under applicable law.

### 3.5 Indirect Data Acquisition

Obtaining measurement data originally produced by Erin's Audio Corner through third-party websites, repositories, APIs, archives, aggregators, or other intermediaries does not, by itself, establish permission to reproduce or commercially redistribute protected content.

Any third-party authorization to host, process, or display measurement data is limited to the terms of the applicable agreement and does not automatically extend to other parties.

Nothing in this section overrides rights lawfully granted under an applicable license or otherwise provided by law.

### 3.6 Circumvention of Access Restrictions

Circumventing authentication requirements, rate limits, technical access controls, or other security measures intended to restrict access to nonpublic measurement datasets or protected website resources.

### 3.7 Interactive Measurement Tools

The availability of interactive measurement graphs, speaker comparison tools, downloadable images, or other visualization features does not grant unrestricted permission to extract underlying numerical datasets or reproduce protected portions of the website's content.

Any authorized export or sharing features remain subject to their applicable terms and permissions.

## 6. Attribution and Source Identification

When referencing measurements produced by Erin's Audio Corner, users are encouraged to:

- Identify Erin Hardison / Erin's Audio Corner as the original measurement source.
- Provide a link to the relevant original review or measurement page.
- Clearly distinguish original measurements from third-party calculations, modifications, or interpretations.
- Avoid presenting modified or reconstructed measurements in a manner that misleadingly suggests endorsement by Erin's Audio Corner.

Attribution alone does not constitute permission for uses that otherwise require authorization.

## 7. Data Accuracy and Third-Party Interpretations

Measurement data can be affected by processing methods, smoothing, normalization, interpolation, digitization accuracy, and other analytical choices.

Third-party reconstruction or modification of measurement data may introduce inaccuracies or lead to conclusions that differ from those of the original measurement source.

Erin's Audio Corner does not endorse or assume responsibility for independently generated graphs, calculated specifications, rankings, or conclusions published by third parties unless expressly stated.

## 8. Third-Party Data Hosting and Distribution

Measurement data may occasionally be supplied to third parties for specific research, educational, collaborative, or publishing purposes.

Such contributions do not automatically grant unrestricted redistribution or commercial-use rights to other individuals or organizations.

Permissions granted under separate agreements or applicable licenses are governed by those agreements or licenses.

This policy does not retroactively revoke permissions that have already been validly granted.

## 9. Requests for Permission

Individuals, researchers, publishers, and organizations interested in licensing measurement data or using protected materials beyond the permissions described above are encouraged to contact Erin's Audio Corner before proceeding.

Requests should describe the intended use, the measurements or materials involved, whether the use is commercial, and how the information would be distributed.

Permission may be granted on a case-by-case basis through written authorization or a separate licensing agreement.

## 10. Policy Updates

Erin's Audio Corner reserves the right to revise this policy as its measurement database, website features, and licensing practices evolve.

Updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date.

Changes to this policy do not automatically modify existing agreements or revoke rights previously granted under valid licenses.
